## 1. Settled Upsizers — *Home movers and downsizers* · **P1**

| Size | Value | Conversion | Decides in | Late payments |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 38,011 customers (37.8%) | 42.5% of deal value · £62,724 per closed lead (index 142) | 17.2% (all 18.2%) | 49 days (all 36) | 2.9% (all 7.6%) |

- **Who:** UK owners moving up or down. Median budget £395k (index 125). 55% buy with a mortgage, 42% in cash. Mostly in the South East.
- **Behaviour:** 57% of sessions on mobile; few virtual tours (3.5%); they look most at the South East.
- **Channels:** Rightmove 32.1%, Zoopla 15.1%, Veltrix website 11.8%, Facebook 7.5%, OnTheMarket 6.6%.
- **Pipeline today:** 1,633 open leads, 750 at viewing or offer. Only 16.8% get a first reply within 1 hour; those that do convert at 29.7%, against 14.0% for later replies.
- **Recommended approach:** route to senior agents on the regional sales desks. First contact by phone, with a branch visit offered (the best-converting first contact, 25.7%). Reply within 1 hour, 7 days a week. This is the first test segment.

## 2. First-Step Buyers — *First-time buyers* · **P2**

| Size | Value | Conversion | Decides in | Late payments |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 21,871 customers (21.7%) | 18.3% of deal value · £37,074 per closed lead (index 84) | 12.3%, the lowest | 60 days, the slowest | 3.7% |

- **Who:** median budget £245k; 97% buy with a mortgage. Mostly in the North West, Yorkshire and the West Midlands.
- **Behaviour:** the most mobile buyers (73% of sessions on mobile); few virtual tours (3.0%).
- **What they ask about:** shared ownership (3.9× as often as average customers), mortgage and finance (2.3×), stamp duty (1.5×).
- **Channels:** Rightmove 28.3%, Zoopla 14.1%, Veltrix website 11.9%, TikTok 11.7%, Instagram 9.7%. Instagram converts best (14.3%).
- **Recommended approach:** mid or senior agents on the regional sales desks, replying within 4 hours. Start every first contact with a mortgage-in-principle referral and a shared-ownership guide; that answers what slows them down.

## 3. Mobile Renters — *UK renters* · **P3**

| Size | Value | Conversion | Decides in | Late payments |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16,818 customers (16.7%) | 1.4% of deal value · £4,379 per closed lead (index 10) | 24.9% | 9 days | 12.3%, the highest |

- **Who:** individual tenants; median rent £1,400 pcm; London first.
- **Behaviour:** 81% of sessions on mobile; they decide within days.
- **Channels:** Rightmove 29.6%, Zoopla 19.5%, Veltrix website 10.7%, Instagram 9.9%, TikTok 9.4%.
- **Payment risk:** 13.8% of their rent is paid late. The problem is the tenancy type, not the people: assured shorthold rent is late 14.0% of the time, company-let rent 1.0%.
- **Recommended approach:** regional lettings desks, an automated reply at once, then same-day follow-up. Direct debit at signing and a reminder 3 days before rent is due.

## 4. Yield Investors — *UK investors and landlords* · **P1**

| Size | Value | Conversion | Decides in | Late payments |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11,902 customers (11.8%) | 25.6% of deal value, 2.2× their share · £52,041 per closed lead (index 118) | 19.5% | 33 days | 3.0% |

- **Who:** portfolio landlords and SPVs. They buy cheaper homes (median £195k, index 62) but many of them: 1.7 enquiries each (index 182). 63% use buy-to-let mortgages, 35% cash.
- **Behaviour:** desktop users (only 33% of sessions on mobile); they look most at the North West.
- **Channels:** Rightmove 22.3%, Veltrix website 15.7%, Referral 13.5%, Email newsletter 13.2%, Zoopla 12.1%. **E-mail is 2% of campaign spend but brings 30% of this segment's campaign leads, at £28 each** (TikTok: £3,814).
- **Recommended approach:** Investor desk, senior agents, a video call as first contact (24.2% conversion), reply within 1 hour. Move spend from TikTok and WeChat into e-mail and Google Ads for this segment.

## 5. Global Buyers — *Overseas buyers and tenants* · **P1**

| Size | Value | Conversion | Decides in | Late payments |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9,462 customers (9.4%) | 11.2% of deal value · £53,367 per closed lead (index 121) | 16.8% | 38 days | 3.9% (8.4% on sale payments) |

- **Who:** live outside the UK (Hong Kong, the UAE, India, Nigeria, Singapore…). Highest budgets: median £523k (index 166). 37% buy in cash.
- **Behaviour:** 24% of their sessions fall between 00:00 and 06:00 UK time (index 506); virtual tours are 16% of their events (index 335); new-build views 71% (index 222).
- **Channels:** overseas agent partners 20.0%, Veltrix website 15.2%, WeChat 11.6%, Rightmove 11.4%, Google Ads 8.7%.
- **Pipeline today:** the slowest first reply of any segment, a median 6.4 hours. Only 8.3% are answered within 1 hour.
- **Recommended approach:** International desk, senior agents. An instant overnight reply with a booking link, then a phone call at the start of their business day. Send virtual tours and new-build stock first.

## 6. Relocation Tenants — *Corporate and relocation tenants* · **P3**

| Size | Value | Conversion | Decides in | Late payments |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2,509 customers (2.5%) | 1.0% of deal value · £17,895 per closed lead (index 40) | 38.9%, the highest | 11 days | 1.5%, the lowest |

- **Who:** company lets and relocations; median rent £2,800 pcm; London.
- **Behaviour:** desktop users; virtual tours 8.7% of events (index 183).

### How the tier was set

Value of an average closed lead = deal value ÷ closed leads, indexed to all leads (100):

- **P1:** index of 115 or more.
- **P2:** 60–114.
- **P3:** under 60.

Agree the thresholds with the sales director before go-live. Source: `clean.segment_tiers`.
